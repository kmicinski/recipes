One zip bag, one bowl, one foil packet — everything on the grill.

## Chicken (zip-bag marinade)
1. In a gallon zip bag: 1/3 cup olive oil, juice of the lemon, 4 smashed garlic
   cloves, 1 Tbsp dijon, 1 tsp salt, lots of black pepper, the oregano, and a
   pinch of the brown sugar. Squish, add thighs, squish again.
2. 30 min on the counter is enough; a few hours in the fridge is better.
3. Grill medium-high, 5-6 min per side, to char + 165°F. Thighs are forgiving —
   err on the char side. Rest 5 min on a foil-lined plate.

## Potatoes (marinated foil packet — no boiling, no smashing)
1. Halve baby potatoes (or chunk regular ones, 1-inch). Toss in a big bowl with:
   1/4 cup olive oil, 1 Tbsp dijon, 1 Tbsp soy sauce (salt + browning — doesn't
   taste like soy), 3 minced garlic cloves, 1 tsp brown sugar, 1/2 tsp pepper,
   1/2 tsp cumin or paprika. Sit 15-30 min while the chicken marinates.
2. Dump everything, marinade included, onto a double layer of heavy foil; seal
   into a packet with a little air space.
3. Grill ~25-30 min over medium, flip once halfway. Fork slides in easy = done.
   Open the packet the last 5 min for crispy edges.
4. Finish with the butter melted in the packet; serve straight from the foil.

## Timing
Potatoes on first, chicken on ~15 min later, both come off together.
Cleanup: one zip bag, one bowl.
