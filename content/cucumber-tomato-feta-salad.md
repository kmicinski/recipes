The cucumber salad dish. Deliberately dressed with the same Italian dressing that marinates the chicken — one bottle, two jobs (pour dressing for the salad BEFORE any goes near raw chicken).

1. Chunk cucumbers and halve tomatoes; slice red onion thin.
2. Toss with dressing; crumble feta over. Black pepper on top.
3. Keeps ~3 days dressed. The plan calls for this at 1.5x — make the second half-batch midweek (5 minutes, no cook).

*Cleanup:* one bowl, one knife.
