Grilled kofta — spiced, juicy, and it freezes beautifully.

1. Grate the red onion straight into a big bowl (discard excess liquid), add minced garlic, spices, and beef. Mix with your hands just until combined — don't overwork.
2. Form into flattened logs around skewers, or simply into **oval patties** (easier, identical flavor, zero technique).
3. Grill over medium-high ~4 min/side to 160°F. Grill the whole batch on prep day.
4. **Meal-prep:** eat half this week (dinner + pita-wrap lunches); freeze the cooked other half flat in a zip bag — that's the second kofta night later in the week, re-warmed in a 350°F oven in foil, 15 min.
5. Serve stuffed in warm pita/naan with tzatziki and cucumber salad.

*Cleanup:* one mixing bowl, grill. If fresh parsley/mint is on hand throw a handful in, but it's not on the shopping list on purpose.
