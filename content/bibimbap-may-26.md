## Instructions

1. Marinate the beef mince with the soy sauce, 1 tbsp sesame oil, brown sugar, and 1/4 tsp of the minced garlic. Set aside for about 30 minutes while you prep the rest.
2. Mix the bibimbap sauce in a bowl: gochujang, 1 tbsp sesame oil, sugar, water, toasted sesame seeds, vinegar, and 1 tsp minced garlic.
3. Cook the spinach and bean sprouts (blanch, squeeze dry, and season each with a little sesame oil, salt, and garlic).
4. Rinse, peel, and julienne the carrots. Heat cooking oil and 1/4 tsp sea salt in a wok and stir-fry on medium-high to high heat for 2-3 minutes.
5. Clean and thinly slice the shiitake mushrooms. Heat cooking oil and 1/4 tsp sea salt in a wok and cook on medium-high to high heat for 2-3 minutes until done.
6. Heat cooking oil in a wok over medium-high to high heat and cook the marinated beef for 3-5 minutes until thoroughly cooked.
7. Fry the eggs (sunny side up is traditional).
8. Divide steamed rice into bowls. Top each with portions of beef, spinach, bean sprouts, carrots, mushrooms, shredded seasoned seaweed, a drizzle of bibimbap sauce, and a fried egg.
9. Mix everything together in the bowl before eating.
