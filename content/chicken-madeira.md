The week's centerpiece — Cheesecake-Factory-style Chicken Madeira, planned for **two dinners** (cook half fresh each night; the sauce and mash carry over, only the green beans and a quick chicken sear are fresh work on night 2).

**Prep-day (night 1):**
1. Halve breasts horizontally, pound thin, salt + pepper. Sear 2 of the 4 cutlet-pairs in olive oil, set aside.
2. Same pan: butter, mushrooms hard-sear until browned, garlic, flour, then Madeira + broth. Reduce ~15 min to a glossy dark sauce. Make the **full double batch of sauce** — it reheats perfectly. (Cheapest bottle of Madeira is fine — it's fortified, so the rest keeps for months for future batches.)
3. Mash: boil potatoes, mash with butter, salt, and a splash of the reserved cooking water. Full double batch; reheats the same way.
4. Return chicken to sauce, top with mozzarella, lid on 2 min to melt. Roast half the green beans alongside (foil-lined pan, 425°F, 10 min, olive oil + salt).

**Night 2 (second prep):** sear the remaining cutlets fresh (5 min), reheat sauce + mash, melt cheese, roast the rest of the beans. Tastes like night one, none of the fatigue.

Gentle-stomach note: the wine cooks fully off; skip the mozzarella on a rough day and it's basically chicken, potatoes, and mushroom broth.
