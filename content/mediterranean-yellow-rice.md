One big pot on prep day; the week's carb base for bowls and plates.

1. Rinse rice. In your largest pot, toast it in olive oil 2 min with turmeric.
2. Add bouillon/stock (4.5 cups liquid), salt. Boil, cover, low 15 min, rest 10 off-heat.
3. Fluff with a fork, squeeze the lemon over, portion into food storage containers while warm.

Reheats perfectly all week (splash of water, microwave). One pot is the entire cleanup.
