The requested Costco-chicken + Italian-dressing-marinade kebabs.

1. Thaw chicken overnight in the fridge. Cube into 1.5" pieces **in a gallon zip bag** (no cutting-board cleanup beyond one board + knife).
2. Pour Italian dressing into the bag, squeeze out air, massage. Marinate 4h-overnight in the fridge.
3. Thread onto metal skewers with chunks of bell pepper and red onion. (Do this on a foil-lined sheet pan — the only thing to wash is the skewers.)
4. Grill over medium-high 12-15 min, turning quarter-way, until 165°F inside.
5. **Meal-prep:** this makes ~3 meals for two. Eat one fresh; slide the rest off the skewers into food storage containers with rice + veg for grab-and-go bowls.

*Cleanup:* zip-bag marinade, foil-lined pan, grill. Never reuse marinade that touched raw chicken — if you want extra dressing as a drizzle, pour it off the fresh bottle first.
