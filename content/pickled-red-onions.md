The whole-week condiment: 10 minutes on prep day, then they brighten the Greek bowls, the chicken salad toasts, and the schnitzel plates all week.

1. Slice onions thin (mandoline or knife).
2. Warm vinegar + water + sugar + salt just until dissolved. Pour over onions in a jar.
3. Ready in an hour, better the next day, keeps 2+ weeks refrigerated.

They only get better as the week goes on — exactly what a prep-day condiment should do.
