Easy day #2 — straight from the Costco freezer bag, and the foil IS the dish.

1. (Ideally thaw fillets in the fridge that morning; from frozen also works, add ~10 min.)
2. Two fillets on a big sheet of foil on a sheet pan: olive oil, salt, oregano, lemon slices on top. Crimp into a sealed packet.
3. Bake 425°F ~15 min (thawed) / ~25 min (frozen) until flaky.
4. Serve over leftover yellow rice with whatever grilled veg and salad remain.

*Cleanup:* throw away the foil. That's it. The rest of the salmon bag stays in the freezer for future weeks.
