The zero-cook easy meal — shredded rotisserie chicken folded into lemony Greek yogurt with dijon, celery, and dill, piled on toasted sourdough with pickled red onions on top.

Uses the other half of the week's rotisserie chicken (the congee takes the first half) — one bird covers both meals.

1. Shred the chicken. Mix with yogurt, dijon, lemon juice, minced celery, dill, salt, pepper.
2. Toast bread. Pile it on. Top with **pickled red onions** from the weekly jar.
3. Salad keeps 3-4 days, so round 2 later in the week is literally just toasting bread.

One bowl, one fork, done in 10 minutes.
