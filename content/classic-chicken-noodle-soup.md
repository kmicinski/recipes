The other gentle classic — same rotisserie chicken as the congee, so one bird covers both.

1. Sweat onion, carrots, celery in olive oil ~8 min. Add broth, simmer 15 min.
2. Stir in shredded rotisserie chicken; season with salt, pepper, a squeeze of lemon, and dill.
3. **Meal-prep trick: cook the egg noodles separately** and store them in their own container — noodles kept in broth turn to mush by day 2. Add a handful to each bowl when reheating.

One pot plus a noodle pot. Freezes well too (broth base only, no noodles).
