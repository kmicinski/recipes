The week's simple dip — uses the same Kirkland Greek yogurt as breakfast and the same English cucumbers as the salad, so it adds zero unique produce.

1. Grate cucumber, squeeze it HARD in your fist (or a paper towel) over the sink.
2. Stir into yogurt with grated garlic, lemon juice, olive oil, dill, salt.
3. **Portion straight into the small dip containers** — one per planned lunch/dinner. Better on day 2+.

No cooking, one bowl, one grater.
