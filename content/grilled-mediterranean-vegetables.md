The vegetable dish — grilled alongside the kebabs on prep day, so the grill does double duty and nothing extra gets dirty.

1. Chunk peppers, zucchini, and onion. Toss right in a grill basket (or a double-foil packet) with olive oil, oregano, salt.
2. Grill over medium 12-15 min, shaking once or twice, until charred at the edges.
3. Portion into containers with the rice — this is the standard bowl: rice + veg + protein + tzatziki + feta.

*Cleanup:* grill basket or just discard the foil packet.
