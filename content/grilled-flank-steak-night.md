Saturday grill night, built around the flank steak already in the freezer — **move it to the fridge Friday morning to thaw.**

1. Zip-bag marinade (morning of): soy sauce, lime juice, grated garlic, olive oil, brown sugar, pepper. Steak in, flip once.
2. Foil-wrapped russets onto the grill first (~45 min, indirect). Corn goes on in the husk for the last 15.
3. Steak over high heat 4-5 min a side to medium-rare. **Rest 10 minutes**, slice thin *against the grain*.
4. Butter + salt for the potatoes and corn at the table.

Grill only half the steak's servings tonight — the rest slices cold into **Sunday's steak sandwiches**: toasted sourdough, pickled red onions from the weekly jar, a swipe of dijon (tzatziki also excellent on it).
