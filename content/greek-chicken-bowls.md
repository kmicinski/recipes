The grill-session meal: lemon-oregano chicken thighs marinated in a **zip bag**, grilled in one batch, then assembled into bowls over the week.

1. Zip-bag marinade: olive oil, lemon juice + zest, oregano, grated garlic, salt, pepper. Thighs in, 30 min to overnight.
2. Grill over medium-high ~5-6 min a side. Rest, slice, refrigerate.
3. Bowls: rice, sliced chicken, cucumber, halved cherry tomatoes, a big spoonful of **tzatziki** and **pickled red onions** (both from the weekly condiment batch — not counted here so the shopping list doesn't double them). The tzatziki brings the creamy-salty note; crumble feta on if there's some around, but it doesn't need it.

Thighs stay juicy reheated, which is why they beat breasts for bowl duty.
