Khao-man-gai-style chicken rice: jasmine rice cooked in chicken broth with smashed ginger, warm shredded rotisserie chicken on top, and a spoonful of ginger-scallion oil that makes the whole thing sing. Gentle on the stomach, ~40g protein a plate, and genuinely craveable.

1. Rice cooked in broth (pot or rice cooker) with 3-4 smashed ginger coins and a pinch of salt. Fish out the ginger after.
2. **Ginger-scallion oil** (the good part): finely mince scallions + grate the rest of the ginger into a heatproof bowl with salt; heat the oil until shimmering and pour it over — it sizzles. Keeps all week, also great on the meatballs.
3. Plate: rice, shredded rotisserie chicken (half the bird — the other half is the chicken salad), spoon of the oil, splash of soy, cool cucumber slices on the side.

Reheats perfectly — chicken and rice together, oil cold from the jar on top. On a rough-stomach day, skip the oil and it's plain chicken, broth rice, and cucumber.
