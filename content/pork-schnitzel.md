Crispy pork schnitzel with lemon, buttered egg noodles, and steamed green beans — planned as **two fresh fry nights** (breaded cutlets hold beautifully; fried ones don't).

**Night 1:**
1. Pound chops to 1/4 inch between plastic wrap (zip bag works). Salt + pepper.
2. Dredge line: flour → beaten egg → panko, pressing the crumbs on. **Bread ALL the cutlets** — fry half tonight, park the rest on a parchment-layered tray in the fridge for night 2.
3. Shallow-fry 2-3 min per side in 1/4 inch of hot cooking oil until deep golden. Drain on a rack or paper towels.
4. Big batch of buttered egg noodles (same bag as the chicken soup — one 16 oz bag covers both); steam green beans alongside.

**Night 2:** cutlets are already breaded — 10 minutes in the pan, reheat noodles, fresh green beans. Lemon wedge over everything, always.

This is the week's indulgent night; if it lands on a rough-stomach day, trade it with a congee or soup day — everything keeps.
