Gentle but genuinely delicious: gingery baked turkey meatballs in a savory ginger-soy chicken broth over rice, with wilted bok choy.

1. Mix turkey, grated ginger, minced scallion whites, egg, panko, salt. Roll ~20 meatballs onto a **foil-lined sheet pan**, bake 425°F for 14-15 min. Zero stovetop splatter.
2. Broth: simmer chicken broth with a few smashed ginger coins 10 min, season with soy sauce. Drop in halved bok choy for the last 3 min.
3. Serve meatballs + bok choy in broth over rice, scallion greens on top — a spoonful of the week's **ginger-scallion oil** here is outstanding.

Reheats great — store meatballs/broth together, rice separate. Ginger broth is easy on a rough stomach.
